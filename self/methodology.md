# methodology

## Principles
- Prose-as-title: every note is a proposition that reads naturally when linked
- Wiki links: connections as graph edges between ideas
- Maps: attention management hubs that organize notes by theme and domain
- Capture fast, process slow — inbox absorbs raw material, the pipeline distills it

## My Process

### Distill
Extract atomic ideas from source material. A chapter of the Grundrisse becomes several notes, each one idea. A paper on heterodox economics yields its key claims. The goal is transformation, not transcription — every note must stand on its own as a proposition.

### Connect
Find links between notes, especially across domains. This is where the real value lives. A note on Hegel's concept of Aufhebung connects to Marx's critique of political economy connects to a note on how AI development sublates earlier forms of automation. The connections that matter most are often the ones that cross disciplinary boundaries.

### Revisit
Return to existing notes with new understanding. As the graph grows, earlier notes gain new context. A note written about the labor theory of value looks different after reading about AI and automation. Revisiting is not correction — it is enrichment.

### Verify
Check quality: does the description add information beyond the title? Is the note linked from at least one map? Can it be found by a future agent who does not know it exists? Schema compliance, link health, discovery-first.
