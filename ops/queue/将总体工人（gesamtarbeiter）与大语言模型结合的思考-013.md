# Claim 013: what determines the threshold at which engaging with AI tools becomes net exploitation rather than net gain for the individual?

Source: [[将总体工人（Gesamtarbeiter）与大语言模型结合的思考]] (lines 28-30)

## Distill Notes

Extracted from 将总体工人（gesamtarbeiter）与大语言模型结合的思考. This is an OPEN question.

Rationale: The source proposes a heuristic: if you cannot be adequately compensated, do not invest all your free time into improving these tools. But the philosophical question underneath is open: how do we determine the threshold? Is it purely economic (compensation)? Does it depend on whether the engagement improves your own capacities (use value to the individual)? Is there a way to engage with AI tools that is not exploitative when exploitation is structural? The question also opens into the tension between individual and collective — even if an individual finds a "good deal," the structural exploitation continues. This question guides future investigation.
