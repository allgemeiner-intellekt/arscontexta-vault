# Claim 003: exploitation under capitalism is not experienced as deprivation but as the natural order of value itself

## Distill Notes

Extracted from 将总体工人（gesamtarbeiter）与大语言模型结合的思考. This is a CLOSED claim.

Rationale: The source articulates a precise distinction between exploitation and deprivation. Exploitation is not robbery (taking from a pre-given value) but the constitution of the value-index itself — it's the social process through which things become valuable. This is why exploitation is not painful: it doesn't violate a natural order, it IS the natural order under capitalism. This is a strong philosophical claim that opens connections to Marxist value theory and ideology critique.
