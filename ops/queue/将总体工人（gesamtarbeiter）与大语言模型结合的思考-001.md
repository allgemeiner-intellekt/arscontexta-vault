# Claim 001: LLMs replace the mental worker's illusion of authorship with the illusion that capital itself is productive

## Distill Notes

Extracted from 将总体工人（gesamtarbeiter）与大语言模型结合的思考. This is a CLOSED claim.

Rationale: This is a core argument about the ideological function of LLMs in the context of cognitive labor. Mental workers under capitalism already have one illusion — that they own and author their products (abstracting away the social relations of production). LLMs introduce a new, historically familiar illusion: that capital itself is productive. The note identifies this as a shift from one mystification to another, both of which have a basis in real social appearances.
