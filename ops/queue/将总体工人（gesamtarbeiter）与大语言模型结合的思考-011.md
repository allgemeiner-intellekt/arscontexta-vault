# Claim 011: the Gesamtarbeiter concept bridges individual mental labor and collective machine-mediated production

Source: [[将总体工人（Gesamtarbeiter）与大语言模型结合的思考]] (lines 6-16)

## Distill Notes

Extracted from 将总体工人（gesamtarbeiter）与大语言模型结合的思考. This is a CLOSED claim.

Rationale: Marx's concept of the Gesamtarbeiter (collective/aggregate worker) dissolves the distinction between individual labor and the collective labor process. The individual mental worker who believes they own their product is in fact contributing to a collective labor process mediated by capital. LLMs make this structural reality vivid: individual mental workers whose outputs (writing, code, ideas) trained the model are now contributors to a collective production apparatus they do not own. The concept is a theoretical tool for making this relation visible.
