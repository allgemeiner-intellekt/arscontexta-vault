# philosophy

German classical philosophy (Kant, Hegel, Fichte, Schelling), phenomenological traditions (Husserl, Heidegger, Merleau-Ponty), and the philosophical foundations that connect to political economy, history, and critical theory.

## Core Notes

### Ideology and Fetishism
- [[both illusions of mental labor are real at different levels of abstraction from social relations]] — ideology as partial truth: how fetishism functions through abstraction rather than simple falsehood
- [[exploitation under capitalism is not experienced as deprivation but as the natural order of value itself]] — the phenomenology of exploitation: why it registers as natural order rather than violation

### Labor and Social Form
- [[the Gesamtarbeiter concept bridges individual mental labor and collective machine-mediated production]] — Marx's collective worker concept and its philosophical implications
- [[Pasquinelli's Italian Operaismo reading of information machines provides the theoretical frame for understanding LLMs as Gesamtarbeiter]] — operaismo's philosophical contribution: cognition as labor

### Epistemology and Inquiry
- [[the questions you ask about a subject reveal your assumptions more than they reveal the subject]] — the frame of inquiry as revealing more than the inquiry itself
- [[rereading a book years later reveals more about how I changed than about what the book says]] — knowledge as indexing the knower
- [[recurring disagreements often stem from different frames rather than different facts]] — frame differences vs. fact differences in dispute
