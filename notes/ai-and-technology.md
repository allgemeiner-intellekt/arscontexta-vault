# AI and technology

Artificial intelligence and its rapid development — both practical (making better use of AI tools) and theoretical (what AI means for labor, knowledge, general intellect). Where technology meets political economy and philosophy.

## Core Notes

### AI as Political Economy
- [[LLMs replace the mental worker's illusion of authorship with the illusion that capital itself is productive]] — ideological function of LLMs: replacing one fetishism with another
- [[LLMs convert internet content into exploitable value by making everything published a productive input]] — the valorization mechanism: all published content becomes productive capital
- [[LLMs are the latest historical case for Babbage's labor theory of machinery and Marx's general intellect]] — LLMs in the long arc of mechanization theory
- [[digital tools occupy a dual character as both machine in the labor process and personal instrument]] — the structural duality of computing tools under capitalism
- [[exploitation through digital tools is structurally unavoidable for individuals under capitalism]] — structural analysis: no individual exit from AI exploitation
- [[individual resistance to AI exploitation is limited; genuine resistance must be collective]] — political consequence: collective action as the appropriate register
- [[Claude Code's evolution from tool to machine mirrors the historical transition from craft to industrial machinery]] — case study: opacity increasing, skill absorbed into apparatus

### AI Use Practice
- [[cost friction in AI use suppresses the iterative refinement that actually develops prompt skill]] — the economic condition of AI use shapes the learning loop; also: structural exploitation even in "free" use
- [[AI responses can be read as diagnostics of the prompt's hidden assumptions]] — misaligned outputs as information about the prompt, not just failure
- [[iterative AI interaction clarifies the human's thinking not just the AI's output]] — prompt iteration as a method for conceptual clarity

### Open Questions
- [[what determines the threshold at which engaging with AI tools becomes net exploitation rather than net gain for the individual]] — unresolved: where does individual benefit end and net exploitation begin?
