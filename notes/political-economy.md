# political economy

Marx's critique of political economy, mainstream economic theory (neoclassical, Keynesian), and heterodox traditions (post-Keynesian, institutionalist, Marxian, world-systems). The intersection of economic theory with philosophy and history.

## Core Notes

### Value Theory and Exploitation
- [[exploitation under capitalism is not experienced as deprivation but as the natural order of value itself]] — exploitation constitutes value rather than violating a prior standard
- [[both illusions of mental labor are real at different levels of abstraction from social relations]] — ideology as partial truth elevated to totality

### Labor and Machinery
- [[the Gesamtarbeiter concept bridges individual mental labor and collective machine-mediated production]] — Marx's collective worker concept dissolves the individual/collective divide
- [[LLMs are the latest historical case for Babbage's labor theory of machinery and Marx's general intellect]] — LLMs in the theoretical arc from Babbage through the Grundrisse
- [[Pasquinelli's Italian Operaismo reading of information machines provides the theoretical frame for understanding LLMs as Gesamtarbeiter]] — operaismo as mediating framework

### Digital Capitalism
- [[LLMs replace the mental worker's illusion of authorship with the illusion that capital itself is productive]] — ideological shift: from authorship mystification to capital-as-productive
- [[LLMs convert internet content into exploitable value by making everything published a productive input]] — total valorization of internet content
- [[digital tools occupy a dual character as both machine in the labor process and personal instrument]] — structural duality of digital tools under capitalism
- [[exploitation through digital tools is structurally unavoidable for individuals under capitalism]] — no individual exit; exploitation is constitutive
- [[individual resistance to AI exploitation is limited; genuine resistance must be collective]] — political conclusion: collective action required

### Open Questions
- [[what determines the threshold at which engaging with AI tools becomes net exploitation rather than net gain for the individual]] — where does individual benefit end and net exploitation begin?
