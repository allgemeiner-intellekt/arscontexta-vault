# rereading a book years later reveals more about how I changed than about what the book says

The experience of returning to a familiar text and finding it different is one of the most reliable mirrors for self-knowledge. The words have not changed, but the reader has. What felt obscure now feels obvious; what felt central now feels peripheral. The delta between readings is a measure of who you became in the interval.

This suggests that understanding is never just about the object — it is always also about the subject encountering it. A book is not a fixed quantity of meaning waiting to be absorbed. It is a surface that reflects different things depending on what the reader brings.
