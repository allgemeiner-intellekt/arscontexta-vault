# AI responses can be read as diagnostics of the prompt's hidden assumptions

A poor AI response is not just a failure to get what you wanted. It is information about what you asked for. When the model goes in an unexpected direction, that direction usually reveals something about the prompt: information was missing, constraints were left implicit, or a key term was underspecified in a way that let the model anchor on a different meaning.

This turns each AI exchange into a two-pass process. The first pass is the AI working on your task. The second pass is you reading the response not for its content, but for what it reveals about your framing. "Why did it go there?" is often a more useful question than "how do I correct it?"

This is a specific instance of a general epistemic principle: a system's response to your input reveals your input's properties. Asking someone a question and watching their confusion locates your question's ambiguity. Watching a machine execute your specification and produce unexpected output locates the specification's gap.
